How to run it

  • Aim for 0.25 to 0.5kg of gain a month. Faster is mostly fat.
  • Hit 1.6 to 2.2g of protein per kilogram of bodyweight, spread across four meals.
  • Add load to the bar every week before you add food to the plate.
  • Take 5g of creatine daily, timing does not matter, consistency does.
  • Sleep seven to nine hours. Growth happens when you are not in the gym.
