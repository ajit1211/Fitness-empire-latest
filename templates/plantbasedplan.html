Getting protein right without meat

The only real challenge in plant-based eating is protein quality. These four habits solve it.

1

Combine your proteins

Pulses with grains, like dal with rice or rajma with roti, give you a complete amino acid profile.

2

Lean on soya and paneer

Soya chunks, tofu and paneer are the densest vegetarian protein sources available cheaply in India.

3

Supplement B12 and D3

Neither is reliably available from plants. A daily multivitamin covers both without fuss.

4

Aim slightly higher on protein

Plant protein digests a little less efficiently, so target the top of your range rather than the middle.

How to run it

  • Hit 1.6 to 2.0g of protein per kilogram of bodyweight, spread across four or five meals.
  • Soak and pressure-cook a large batch of pulses once a week so protein is never the hard part.
  • Keep iron absorption up by pairing dal and greens with something citrus.
  • Swap dairy for fortified soya milk if you are eating fully vegan.
  • Review your weight and lifts every four weeks and adjust portions, not the food list.
