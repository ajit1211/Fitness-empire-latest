How to run it

  • Eat protein at every meal. It is the single biggest factor in keeping muscle during a cut.
  • Weigh yourself once a week, same day, same time, before eating.
  • Expect 0.5 to 0.75kg a week. Faster than that and you are losing muscle too.
  • Keep two meals a week flexible so the plan survives real life.
  • Train with weights three to four times a week. Cardio supports the deficit, it does not replace it.
